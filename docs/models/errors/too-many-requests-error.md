# TooManyRequestsError

Read error.quotaKind. credits: nothing is left to spend; add money at error.upgradeUrl. On a plan, error.retryAfter is when the monthly allowance refills. With error.details naming the workspace's five-hour usage limit ("search_workspace_window_budget"), every paid read resumes after error.retryAfter, or at once with money added by card at error.upgradeUrl, which no limit holds. With error.details naming the trial's included usage ("trial_provider_cost_cap"), money added by card at error.upgradeUrl goes on at once, and starting the plan ends the cap. Any other kind: wait error.retryAfter seconds.
