# PublicCompanyTooManyRequests

Read quotaKind. credits: nothing is left to spend; add money at upgradeUrl. On a plan, retryAfter is when the monthly allowance refills. With details naming the workspace's five-hour usage limit ("search_workspace_window_budget"), every paid read resumes after retryAfter, or at once with money added by card at upgradeUrl, which no limit holds. With details naming the trial's included usage ("trial_provider_cost_cap"), money added by card at upgradeUrl goes on at once, and starting the plan ends the cap. Any other kind: wait retryAfter seconds. The body is the flat `{ error: "<text>" }` form; a few answers use the envelope.
