## Overview

Public data: people, companies and posts from our search

* [publicProfile](#publicprofile) - Read a public profile
* [publicFindPeople](#publicfindpeople) - Find people from public data
* [publicFindCompanies](#publicfindcompanies) - Find companies from public data

## publicProfile

Read a professional profile, or up to 25 in one call, and save each as an enriched contact. One profile is read on preview and several in full, unless detail says otherwise. Preview gives the name, current employer, place, photo, follower count, the opening of the About and the latest posts, in about 3 seconds. Full gives the real headline, the current title with its start date, the dated career, schools, place and country, the About, languages and the connection and follower counts, usually live (readAt says when an older copy was used), in about 2 seconds a person. Your own profile is read in full and never saved. This is the default way to look up or enrich a person. Cost: 0,005 € a person read, on preview or full, found or not; nothing for a person a search already returned or one that could not be read. Prices are quoted on Pro; pay as you go costs 30% more. spent is what the call cost at the user's own rate, in money: tell the user that amount as it is, never creditsUsed.

## publicFindPeople

Find people from public profiles: found profiles save as contacts and render as a people list you narrate with a one-line count. One ask is one call: every plausible title, seniority or city as an array on the SAME field, any headline signal in filters.keywords, limit (1-100, default 15) set by the number or volume word they used, sentence added when they named one person rather than a crowd, and more:true, excludeWords or excludeProfiles only to continue or refine a search that already ran. When companies to leave out or a company size are set, the answer says how many people it left out: say so. One person asked for by name is looked up directly and kept only when the name is theirs; otherwise the people come from our index, about 3 weeks old at the median, so a recent job move can be missing; a search answers in about 2 seconds, and one in ten takes about 10 or more. Cost: 0,005 € a person found, their profile included; a search that finds nobody new costs one person. Prices are quoted on Pro; pay as you go costs 30% more. spent is what the call cost at the user's own rate, in money: tell the user that amount as it is, never creditsUsed.

## publicFindCompanies

No people come back. When a company size, a funding window or companies to leave out are set, the answer says how many companies it left out: say so. The companies come from our index, so a recent change to a company can be missing. Cost: 0,005 € a company found; a search that finds none costs one company. Prices are quoted on Pro; pay as you go costs 30% more. spent is what the call cost at the user's own rate, in money: tell the user that amount as it is, never creditsUsed.

## publicFindEmployees

Give the company by name, website or page address, and one or more roles in keywords to narrow to whoever holds one of them. A company given by its website, with no keywords and no sentence, returns people checked to work there now; otherwise the people come from our index, about 3 weeks old at the median and older for some people, so a job they have left can still show. Cost: 0,005 € a person found, their profile included; nothing for a person already in your contacts; more pays only for the people not returned before; a search that finds nobody new costs one person. Prices are quoted on Pro; pay as you go costs 30% more. spent is what the call cost at the user's own rate, in money: tell the user that amount as it is, never creditsUsed.

## publicCompany

Read a public company page: identity, description, tagline, logo, follower and employee counts, website, head office, industry and founded year, recent posts, and the pages it shows beside it. Recent posts can be mined for people with collect_engagers. Up to date, with industry and founded year, in about 2 seconds, a few more when the company is named rather than given by its page address. Cost: 0,005 € for the company with its industry and founded year, the same when no company is found; the same company again within a week is free. Prices are quoted on Pro; pay as you go costs 30% more. spent is what the call cost at the user's own rate, in money: tell the user that amount as it is, never creditsUsed.

## publicFindSignals

Two steps from public data. mode events returns dated events (a raise, an appointment, a job opening) for a description of the event, each with its title, date, source page and an id. mode resolve takes companies read in those events, each with a role to reach or the person the event names, and returns the company's LinkedIn page and the people found, each with their profile and the event. A company whose page is not found returns nobody. Events and people already returned never come back; an events ask with includeText keeps its own record, so it can return an event a wider ask already gave. The people found are saved to the list unless save is skip. Each event carries its date; the people come from our index, about 3 weeks old at the median, so a recent job move can be missing. Cost: in mode events, 0,025 € an event found; in mode resolve, nothing more for a company whose event was returned here, 0,025 € for any other company answered, and 0,05 € for a named person found; a call that finds nothing costs 0,025 €. Prices are quoted on Pro; pay as you go costs 30% more. spent is what the call cost at the user's own rate, in money: tell the user that amount as it is, never creditsUsed.

## runSearches

Run a ready plan returned by plan-searches, in the background, and save the people it finds into the conversation's list, or your contacts outside one. Answers within about 25 seconds with a receipt: people added so far, what it has spent so far (spent, in money; credits_used for code), and each search's state. A run answers in about 6 seconds, and one in ten takes about 10 or more; the people come from our index, about 3 weeks old at the median, so a recent job move can be missing. A plan runs once: calling again with the same plan_id answers where its run stands and spends nothing more. Every search is paid from the workspace balance like a people search, and the run never spends more than the plan's cost_estimate. With an API key the receipt also lists the people added since the last answer (people, at most 200 per answer; call again with the same plan_id for the rest), and GET /contacts/search with planId reads the run's people back for seven days after the run.
